Delete your AI Interview account

You can delete your account and data yourself, at any time, in the app or on this website.

Steps

  1. Sign in to AI Interview, in the app or at this website.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Scroll to Delete account, enter your password and press Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.
Open my profile

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Name, email, password, profile detailsDeleted immediately
Every interview: transcript, scores, feedbackDeleted immediately
Consent records and sign-in sessionsDeleted immediately
Company workspace membershipsRemoved. A workspace you own alone is closed

What is kept

Security log entries and interview-credit records are kept. They hold internal reference numbers only and no longer identify you once your account is deleted.

If you own a company workspace that still has other members, remove them first; the app will tell you if this applies.
