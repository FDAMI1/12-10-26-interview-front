Privacy Policy

Aivarnix · Last updated 13 October 2026

Aivarnix lets you practise job interviews with an AI interviewer and receive a scored report. This policy explains what collects when you use the app or website, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name and email addressTo create your account and let you sign in
Password (stored only as a one-way hash)To sign you in
Target roles, experience level and skills you enterTo tailor interview questions
Interview transcripts: the questions asked and the answers you giveTo run the interview, score it and show you your report
Scores and written feedbackTo show you your report
Your consent choice and the time you gave itTo prove you agreed before an interview started
Sign-in sessions and a security log of account actionsTo keep accounts secure

What we do not collect

  • We do not record audio or video of you. If you dictate an answer, your browser turns your speech into text on your device or through your browser's own speech service; only the text reaches us.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not show advertising and do not sell your data.
  • We do not try to infer age, gender, ethnicity, health, emotion or any other protected characteristic.

Who processes your data

We use these service providers to run the app. They process data only to provide their service to us.

ProviderWhat they receive
Google (Gemini API)Your answers and the interviewer's questions, to generate the next question, your scores and feedback; and the interviewer's lines, to turn them into speech
ElevenLabs (when enabled)The interviewer's lines only, to turn them into speech. Your answers are not sent
Our hosting and database providersAll of the data listed above, to store and serve it

Company interviews

If a company invites you to an interview, authorised members of that company's workspace can read your transcript, scores and feedback for that interview. You are told this, and asked to consent, before the interview starts.

AI feedback

Scores and feedback are generated automatically by AI from your transcript. They are meant for practice and assessment support. They are not an employment decision.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and interviews until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, every interview with its transcript and scores, your consent records and your workspace memberships straight away. Security logs and credit records that contain no personal details may be kept.

Your choices

  • You can decline consent before an interview; it is cancelled, no interview data is collected, and only the fact that you declined is recorded.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time. See how to delete your account.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or for a correction, by contacting us.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Access to interviews is limited to you, to the company that invited you where that applies, and to platform administrators.

Children

Aivarnix is intended for people preparing for employment and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and you are asked for consent again before your next interview.
